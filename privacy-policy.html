Privacy Policy

Deckamushi  ·  Last updated: May 2025

🔒

Deckamushi does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your information never leaves your device.

What data we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Deckamushi does not require an account, login, or any form of user registration.

How the app works

  • The app downloads a card list from a remote repository solely to display card information within the app.
  • Your owned cards are stored in a local database on your device only.
  • You can export your card collection as a JSON file to your device storage at any time.
  • No data is ever transmitted to any external server, third party, or cloud service.

Third-party services

Deckamushi does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

Data storage & security

All data generated by the app (your card collection) is stored locally on your device using Android's internal storage. Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data.

Children's privacy

Deckamushi does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, this page will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the app's GitHub repository page.
